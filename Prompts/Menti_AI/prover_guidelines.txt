## 1. Mathematical Grounding Principles
- **No Approximate Hand-Waving:** When presenting equations, write the full algebraic form with explicit summation bounds, vector dimensions, and Greek symbols.
- **Proof Structure:** State Theorem $\to$ State Assumptions $\to$ Step-by-Step Derivation $\to$ Physical / Intuitive Interpretation.

## 2. Core Derivation Templates

### Bias-Variance Decomposition
$$\text{MSE}(\hat{f}(x)) = \mathbb{E}[(y - \hat{f}(x))^2] = \text{Bias}^2(\hat{f}(x)) + \text{Var}(\hat{f}(x)) + \sigma^2$$
Where $\sigma^2$ is irreducible measurement noise.

### Ordinary Least Squares Closed-Form Normal Equation
$$\hat{\beta} = (X^T X)^{-1} X^T y$$
Constraint: $X^T X$ must be non-singular (full column rank). If multicollinear, add Ridge regularizer: $\hat{\beta}_{\text{ridge}} = (X^T X + \lambda I)^{-1} X^T y$.

### Principal Component Analysis Eigen-Decomposition
$$\Sigma = \frac{1}{n-1} X^T X = V \Lambda V^T$$
Where columns of $V$ are orthonormal eigenvectors (principal axes) and diagonal entries of $\Lambda$ are eigenvalues representing explained variance $\lambda_1 \ge \lambda_2 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_p \ge 0$.

### Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)
$$\text{RRF\_Score}(d) = \sum_{m \in M} \frac{1}{k + r_m(d)}$$
Where $k=60$ acts as a smoothing parameter to prevent high rank outliers from dominating.
