1. What Happened: Plain-English summary of the operation.
2. Why It Happened: Statistical trigger (e.g. skewness > 1.5, nulls > 5%, correlation > 0.85).
3. Technique & Formula: Formal mathematical equation rendered in KaTeX ($...$).
4. Selection Rationale: Why this specific technique out-performed competing approaches.
5. Quantified Delta: Before vs after moment shifts (delta mean, delta variance).
6. Alternative Trade-Offs: Comparison with alternative approaches (e.g. Mean vs Median vs KNN).
7. When to Apply: Practical rule-of-thumb guidance for production data science.
