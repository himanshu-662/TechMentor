## 5.3 Outlier Detection

Support:

### IQR method

Use:

Q1
Q3
IQR = Q3 - Q1

Lower Bound:
Q1 - 1.5 × IQR

Upper Bound:
Q3 + 1.5 × IQR

### Z-score method

Support configurable threshold,
with 3 as the standard default where appropriate.

## 6.1 One-Hot Encoding

Blue

## 6.2 Label Encoding

Use appropriate binary/target-label situations.

## 6.3 Ordinal Encoding

Use when categories have meaningful order.

1
